What Is the Premier League?

The Premier League is the highest level of professional soccer in England. It started in 1992 and is one of the most popular leagues in the world.

Every season, 20 clubs compete for the title. Fans enjoy the exciting matches, famous players, and competitive teams.

Premier League Quick Facts

20 Teams

Twenty clubs play in the league each season.

38 Games

Each club plays 38 league matches.

3 Points

Teams earn three points for a win.

How Does It Work?

The season usually runs from August to May. Every team plays the other teams twice, once at home and once away.

Win: 3 points

Draw: 1 point

Loss: 0 points

At the end of the season, the club with the most points wins the league. The bottom three clubs are relegated to the Championship.
