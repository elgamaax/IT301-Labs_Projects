What Is the Champions League?

The UEFA Champions League brings together top soccer clubs from different European countries to compete against each other.

Unlike the Premier League, which includes only English clubs, the Champions League features teams from leagues across Europe.

How Does It Work?

The competition has 36 clubs in its league phase. Each club plays eight matches against different opponents.

Top 8: Go directly to the round of 16.

9th-24th: Play in the knockout playoffs.

25th-36th: Are eliminated.

Stages of the Champions League

Clubs must advance through several stages to win the trophy.

  1. League Phase

    Teams earn points to qualify for the next round.

  2. Knockout Playoffs

    Clubs placed 9th through 24th compete for a spot in the round of 16.

  3. Round of 16

    Sixteen clubs compete to reach the quarterfinals.

  4. Quarterfinals

    Eight clubs remain in the competition.

  5. Semifinals

    Four clubs compete for two places in the final.

  6. Final

    The last two clubs compete for the trophy.

Famous Champions League Clubs

Some well-known clubs include:

  • Real Madrid
  • FC Barcelona
  • Bayern Munich
  • Liverpool
  • AC Milan

Why Is It So Popular?

Fans get to watch some of the best teams and players in the world compete against each other.

It creates exciting matchups between clubs from different countries.
